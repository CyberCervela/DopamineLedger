# Privacy Policy — Dopamine Ledger

_Last updated: 2026-05-22_

This is the privacy policy for **Dopamine Ledger**, a personal time-credit
tracking app for iOS.

The short version: **the app does not collect, transmit, or share any
of your data.** Everything stays on your device.

## What data is stored

Dopamine Ledger stores the following on-device, in the app's private
SwiftData database:

- Activities you create (names, rates, icons, kinds)
- Quests you create (names, payoff amounts, completion status)
- Sessions you start and stop (timestamps)
- Your credit balance and debt records
- App preferences (e.g. one-time flags for the first-launch
  experience)

This data never leaves your iPhone. We have no servers. We have no
analytics. We have no third-party SDKs.

## What data is _not_ collected

- We do not collect personally identifiable information (name, email,
  device ID, IDFA, etc.)
- We do not use analytics services (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel,
  no Firebase, no Crashlytics)
- We do not communicate with any server we operate or any third party
- We do not track usage across other apps or services
- We do not use cookies (the app has no web view)

## iOS permissions

The app requests one permission via iOS:

- **Notifications** — used solely to deliver a local "5-minute
  warning" and "balance hit zero" alarm during a spender session.
  These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS on
  your device. We never send a push notification from a server.

You can revoke this permission at any time in **Settings → Notifications
→ Dopamine Ledger**.

## Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your iPhone, the app's data may
be included in your encrypted iCloud Backup, per Apple's standard iOS
backup policy. This is between you and Apple — we have no access to
your iCloud account or backups.

## Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time:

- **Inside the app**: Settings → Reset → "Reset balance to 0" and
  "Clear all debt". Activities can be deleted by swiping a row.
- **From iOS**: long-press the app icon → Remove App → Delete App.
  This permanently erases the on-device database.

## Children

The app is designed for ages 4+ and contains no content rated otherwise.
It does not collect any data from any user, including children.
